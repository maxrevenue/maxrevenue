Free beginner checklist

Your OneClick Fortune setup map

A short order of operations for setting up a simple online offer. Follow the steps in order — do not skip ahead to traffic or upsells.

1. Pick one offer type

  1. Choose a single beginner-friendly offer (digital guide, checklist, or simple service).
  2. Write one sentence: who it helps + the outcome in plain English.
  3. Ignore niche-hopping until this first offer is live.

2. Assemble the minimum stack

  1. Page builder / template for the offer page.
  2. Checkout or payment link for the paid kit.
  3. Email capture for this free map (you’re on it).
  4. A thank-you page with the next clear step.

3. Customize in this order

  1. Headline and promise (clarity over cleverness).
  2. What they get / what happens after purchase.
  3. Price and refund language.
  4. Opt-in copy on the free guide page.
  5. Only then: traffic tests (one channel at a time).

4. 7-day review dashboard

  1. Visitors to /squeeze
  2. Opt-in rate
  3. Clicks to the $17 training kit
  4. Purchases / refunds
  5. One note: what confused people this week

This map is educational. Results vary. When you want the full training kit and software access, continue to the offer page.
